### 1. **Movement and Foraging Behavior**
- **Directed movement**: Instead of random steps, rabbits could move toward areas with higher grass density (using perception). You could implement a simple "sense" radius where they check neighboring cells for grass and move toward the richest patch.
- **Memory and learning**: Rabbits could remember locations of good food sources and return to them.
- **Obstacles and habitat preference**: Introduce barriers (e.g., rocks, water) or preferred terrain (e.g., open fields vs. forest) that affect movement speed or energy cost.
- **Resting behavior**: Rabbits might rest during certain times (e.g., night) to conserve energy.

**Example (directed movement)**:
```python
def find_best_grass(self):
    neighbors = self.model.grid.get_neighbors(self.pos, moore=True, radius=2, include_center=False)
    best_grass = None
    max_energy = 0
    for agent in neighbors:
        if isinstance(agent, Grass) and agent.grown:
            # could also consider distance
            if agent.energy_value > max_energy:  # if grass has variable energy
                max_energy = agent.energy_value
                best_grass = agent.pos
    return best_grass
```

### 2. **Energy and Metabolism**
- **Variable food quality**: Grass could have different energy content based on regrowth stage, season, or soil quality.
- **Activity-based metabolism**: Energy cost could depend on movement speed, terrain, or activity (e.g., running from predator costs more).
- **Energy storage**: Rabbits could store excess energy as fat and use it during scarcity.
- **Thirst and water**: Add a water need; rabbits must drink periodically.

**Example (grass with variable energy)**:
```python
class Grass(mesa.Agent):
```

- **Mating behavior**: Require two rabbits of opposite sex (if you add sex) to reproduce, rather than asexual division.
- **Age at maturity**: Rabbits should not reproduce until a certain age.
- **Litter size**: Give birth to multiple offspring (not just one) with variation.
- **Parental investment**: Offspring could stay with mother for some time, dependent on her for energy.
- **Seasonal breeding**: Reproduction only occurs in certain seasons.

### 5. **Environment and Resources**
- **Spatial heterogeneity**: Grass regrowth rate, energy, or carrying capacity varies across the grid (e.g., fertile vs. barren patches).
- **Seasonal cycles**: Change grass growth, temperature, or day length affecting metabolism and reproduction.
- **Disturbances**: Add random events like fire, drought, or human activity that remove grass or kill rabbits.
- **Carrying capacity**: Limit total population based on available grass, not just energy.

**Example (seasonal grass regrowth)**:
```python
class Grass(mesa.Agent):
    def step(self):
        if not self.grown:
            self.countdown -= 1
            if self.countdown <= 0:
                # Regrowth depends on season
                season = self.model.current_season
                if season == 'spring':
                    self.grown = True
                elif season == 'winter':
                    # slower regrowth
                    if self.model.random.random() < 0.1:
                        self.grown = True
```
